Discovery questions concerned within-field crop profitability from yield maps, spatially variable economic returns, published per-acre cost assumptions, and the distinction between cash margins and total economic profit. Consensus returned the Massey study (record `028bb446c95b583785c60cf36a35ca62`); SciSpace identified the Kinoshita study and related spatial-profitability work. The verified primary records above, rather than generated search summaries, anchor the methods discussion.

The budget-selection rule is a stated analytical choice: original production year, dryland corn after soybean prioritized, with tillage and geographic mismatches explicitly retained. It is not inferred from these papers. Economic results are conditional modeled returns; FINBIN remains a separately labeled operator-account proxy. Continuous profit is an affine transformation of yield where price, operator share and cost are spatially uniform, so its NCCPI correlation supplies no independent profitability validation.
